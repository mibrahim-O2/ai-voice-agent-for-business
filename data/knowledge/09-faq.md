# Frequently asked questions

**Are your technicians licensed and insured?**
Yes. All Oakleaf technicians are licensed and insured (fictional demo company).

**Do you give free estimates?**
Estimates for new installations are free. Repairs start with a $89 diagnostic visit, which is
waived if the repair is done on the same visit.

**Which brands do you install?**
Oakleaf installs most major heating and cooling brands and will recommend options during the estimate.

**How often should I service my system?**
Heating and cooling systems should be serviced once a year. The annual maintenance plan covers
one heating and one cooling tune-up.

**Can I talk to a person?**
Yes. Ask the AI assistant at any time and it will hand the call to the office coordinator during
business hours, or take a message for a call back.
