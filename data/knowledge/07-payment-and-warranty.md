# Payment

- Accepted: credit and debit cards, bank transfer and cheque. Cash is not accepted.
- Payment is due when the work is complete. Installations over $2,000 require a 30% deposit.
- Financing is available for installations over $1,000, subject to approval by a partner lender.
- The AI assistant never takes card numbers over the phone. Payment links are sent by the office.

# Warranty

- Repairs: 90-day labour warranty.
- Installations: 1-year labour warranty, plus the manufacturer's parts warranty.
- Maintenance plan members get a 10% discount on repairs.
