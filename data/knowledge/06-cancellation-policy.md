# Cancellation and rescheduling

- Customers can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment carry a $40 late-cancellation fee.
- If the technician arrives and nobody is home, a $60 missed-visit fee applies.
- Cancellations requested through the AI assistant are reviewed by a human coordinator before the
  appointment is released.
