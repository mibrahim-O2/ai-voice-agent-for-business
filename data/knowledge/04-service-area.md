# Service area

Oakleaf serves the fictional towns of Maple Hollow, Riverbend and Cedar Point, and anywhere within
25 miles of the Maple Hollow office.

Visits between 25 and 40 miles away are possible with a $45 travel fee. Oakleaf does not travel
more than 40 miles. Callers outside the area are offered a referral list by email.
