# Privacy and AI-assisted calls

- Calls are answered by an AI assistant. It says so at the start of every call.
- Callers can ask for a human at any time.
- Call details are used only to handle the enquiry and book service. They are never sold.
- Customers can ask for their details to be deleted by emailing privacy@example.com.
- Sensitive actions (creating or changing a customer record, booking or cancelling an
  appointment) are approved by a human before they take effect.
