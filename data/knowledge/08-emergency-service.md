# Emergency service

Emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for:

- no heat when the outside temperature is below 40°F,
- active water leaks or burst pipes,
- sparking outlets or a burning smell from electrical equipment.

The emergency call-out fee is $175, plus the cost of the repair.

If a caller smells gas, they must leave the building immediately and call their gas utility's
emergency line or 911 from outside. The AI assistant must not try to troubleshoot a gas leak.
