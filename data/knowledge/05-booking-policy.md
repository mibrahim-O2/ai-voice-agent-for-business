# Booking an appointment

- Appointments are one hour long and are booked into the next available slot that suits the customer.
- To book, we need the customer's name, a phone number or email, the service needed, and the address.
- The AI assistant can offer open slots, but every booking is confirmed by a human coordinator
  before it is final. The customer receives a confirmation message after approval.
- Same-day appointments are only offered for emergencies.
- A technician arrives within a one-hour arrival window that starts at the slot time.
