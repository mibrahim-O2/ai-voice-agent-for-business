# Business hours

- Office and phone line: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Central Time).
- Appointments: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The last appointment starts at 4:00 PM.
- Lunch break: no appointments start between 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM.
- Saturday and Sunday: closed. Messages left over the weekend are returned on Monday morning.
- Public holidays: closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

Emergency service has its own hours. See the emergency service document.
