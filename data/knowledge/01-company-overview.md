# Oakleaf Home Services (fictional demo company)

Oakleaf Home Services is a fictional heating, cooling, plumbing and electrical company used only
to demonstrate this AI voice agent. It does not exist.

Oakleaf serves homeowners and small businesses. The team has two field technicians and one office
coordinator. Calls are answered first by an AI voice assistant, which can answer questions,
collect details for a new enquiry, and offer appointment times. A human coordinator reviews
bookings, cancellations and changes to customer records before they are final.
